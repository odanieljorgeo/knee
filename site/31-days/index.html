Thirty-one days.

One prompt a day, one verse, one prayer. Check the day off when you have prayed it. If you miss a day, start again on the next one — there is no penalty here and no streak worth shaming yourself over.

0 days prayed

Tap any day to open it. Your progress stays on this device only — we never see it, and clearing your browser data erases it.

Day 1 – 31

Day 1 · The Fire

Tell God where it fell

God, prayer went quiet somewhere and You know where. I am starting here.

Psalm 51:10

Day 2 · The Target

Name who you have been fighting

Lord, I have been swinging at a person. Take the fight out of my hands.

Ephesians 6:12

Day 3 · The Name

One sentence Scripture says about you

Father, let me hear Your voice more clearly than the accusation.

Ephesians 1:4–5

Day 4 · The House

One person under your roof, by name

God, I bring my house to You by name, not as a category.

Joshua 24:15

Day 5 · The Ledger

The page that opens at three in the morning

Lord, You closed this case. Teach me to stop reopening it.

Psalm 103:12

Day 6 · The Night

Say the fear out loud

God, here it is, named instead of circling. Stand at the gate of my mind.

Philippians 4:6–7

Day 7 · The Eyes

One decision made early

God, make my private life match my public one.

Psalm 101:3

Day 8 · The Weight

Say the number out loud

Father, give me enough for today, and loosen my grip.

Matthew 6:11

Day 9 · The Wound

Name what it cost you

Lord, do in me what I cannot do myself. I hand this person over.

Ephesians 4:32

Day 10 · The Table

Bless the person you are divided from

Jesus, bless them — genuinely, not as a strategy. Take my need to be right.

Romans 12:18

Day 11 · The House

Pray in the room while they sleep

Build this house, because I have been building it in my own strength.

Psalm 127:1

Day 12 · The Fire

Pray on a day you feel nothing

God, I feel nothing today and I came anyway. Make the iron float.

2 Kings 6:6

Day 13 · The Name

Say it out loud in your own voice

Father, I agree with what You have already recorded about me.

1 John 3:1

Day 14 · The Target

Pray for them before you speak to them

Lord, give me restraint. Let me bring this to You first.

2 Corinthians 10:4

Day 15 · The Night

One true thing about God, not your situation

God, You do not change when my week does. That is enough tonight.

Isaiah 41:10

Day 16 · The Weight

Give something small away

Father, loosen my grip while I am short. Provide, and keep me generous.

Philippians 4:19

Day 17 · The Ledger

Confess specifically, once

Lord, I name it plainly and I am not going to relitigate it.

1 John 1:9

Day 18 · The Wound

Hand them over again

Lord, I am handing this over again today, and that is not failure.

Colossians 3:13

Day 19 · The House

Ask what your part is

God, show me my part plainly. I have been good at seeing theirs.

Deuteronomy 6:6–7

Day 20 · The Eyes

Tell one safe person the truth

God, take the secrecy. That is the engine, and I want it gone.

Matthew 5:8

Day 21 · The Fire

Thank Him for three ordinary things

God, before I ask for anything, here are three things I had stopped noticing.

Psalm 42:1

Day 22 · The Table

Make the call you have been composing

Lord, give me humility to move first without waiting to be met halfway.

John 17:21

Day 23 · The Night

Hand over the fear about someone else

God, my fear for them is not helping either of us. Take it.

1 Peter 5:7

Day 24 · The Name

Conviction or accusation? Check the tone

Father, teach me the difference between Your voice and the accusation.

Romans 8:15

Day 25 · The Weight

Ask for today, not the whole year

Father, today. Just today. You said that was the right size to ask.

Hebrews 13:5

Day 26 · The Target

Pray a blessing over the difficult person

Jesus, bless them. You have already won this; help me act like it.

Colossians 2:15

Day 27 · The Ledger

Sleep like someone who is forgiven

Lord, there is no condemnation. Let my body believe that tonight.

Romans 8:1

Day 28 · The House

Write what has changed — in them or in you

God, here is what moved this month. Some of it moved in me.

3 John 1:4

Day 29 · The Table

Pray for someone one season behind you

Lord, show me one person I could walk with for six weeks.

Psalm 133:1

Day 30 · The Wound

Keep from becoming what hurt you

Father, forgive them. And keep me from becoming what hurt me.

Luke 23:34

Day 31 · The Fire

Decide about tomorrow

God, day thirty-one is not the end. I will be here tomorrow.

Matthew 6:6
