Privacy.

The short version: we do not collect anything about you.

No accounts, ever

There is no login, no registration, and no email list on this site. You cannot create an account because there is nothing to create one for.

Your writing stays on your device

Prayer cards, the 31-day tracker, and assessment results are stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot read them. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently, and we cannot recover them.

No tracking or advertising cookies

We do not use advertising networks, remarketing pixels, social trackers, or third-party analytics that profile individuals. Our hosting provider records standard, aggregate server request data for security and reliability.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of serving the file. That is the only third-party request the site makes.

If you email us

Messages sent to info@cbaorlando.org are read by people at CBA Orlando and are kept confidential. We will not add you to any list, and we will not share your message.

Children

This site is written for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect information at all.
