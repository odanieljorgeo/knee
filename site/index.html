What a knee battle is

Not a mood. A decision, made in a specific place, about a specific fight.

A knee battle is what happens when you stop rehearsing arguments in the shower and start bringing the actual fight — named, out loud, on purpose — to God. It usually needs three things people rarely have at the same time: somewhere to do it, something to say, and a reason to come back tomorrow. That is all this site is.

A place

A closet, a corner, a parked car. Ten minutes and almost no money. We will show you exactly what goes on the wall and why.
