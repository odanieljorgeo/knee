Books that go deeper

We did not write these and we do not sell them. They are simply good.

Fervent — Priscilla Shirer

The book behind the film most people know. A practical, strategy-based approach to prayer.

Prayer — Timothy Keller

The most thorough modern treatment: theology and practice together, without sentimentality.

The Practice of the Presence of God — Brother Lawrence

A seventeenth-century monk who prayed while washing dishes. Very short, very old, still unmatched.

A Praying Life — Paul Miller

Especially good if prayer has become discouraging or you feel you are bad at it.
